Asgardian Village is a merit-based community of makers ("a Canadian house of makers": hydroponics, 3D-printed
modular nodes, veteran outreach, a direct-diplomacy doctrine) that publishes an A2A agent card for humans and AI
agents. The site is a Grok App hosted by xAI on the asgardian-village.grok.me subdomain; the registrable domain
grok.me belongs to xAI and redirects to grok.com.
